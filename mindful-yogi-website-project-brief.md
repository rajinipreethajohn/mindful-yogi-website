# Mindful Yogi Website Project Brief

## Purpose

Build a warm, booking-ready website for **Mindful Yogi**, a yoga teaching and wellness brand led by Preetha. The site should quickly explain who Preetha helps, what visitors can book, how online sessions work, and how Mindful Yogi connects movement, breath, reflection, philosophy, and educational products.

The first version should feel useful and alive, not like a generic wellness landing page.

## Brand Positioning

**Mindful Yogi** offers gentle, grounded yoga for real life: movement, breathwork, reflection, and yogic philosophy made accessible for modern students.

Position Preetha as:

- a calm and approachable yoga teacher;
- a guide for beginners and returning students;
- someone who supports stress relief, mobility, women’s wellbeing, and steady practice without making medical claims;
- someone rooted in Indian yogic tradition while speaking clearly to a contemporary, international audience;
- someone who offers both practice-based sessions and reflective philosophy content.

Suggested positioning line:

> Yoga for steadiness, strength, breath, and everyday inner balance.

Other possible lines:

- Gentle yoga, breathwork, and philosophy for modern life.
- Rooted yoga guidance for body, breath, and mind.
- Mindful movement and yogic reflection with Preetha.

## Visual Theme

Use a calm, natural visual system:

- colors: sand, warm beige, clay, soft brown, muted green, off-white;
- imagery: natural light, mats, plants, woven textures, calm interiors, realistic people practicing yoga;
- typography: readable body font plus an elegant display font with a subtle Indian or Devanagari-inspired feeling if available;
- mood: grounded, personal, spacious, warm, culturally aware;
- UI: clean sections, soft edges, good mobile spacing, clear booking buttons.

Avoid:

- loud fitness/gym branding;
- medical-clinic aesthetics;
- heavy purple gradients;
- vague stock silhouettes;
- exaggerated spiritual or decorative cliches.

## Homepage Goals

The homepage should answer five questions immediately:

1. What is Mindful Yogi?
2. Who is it for?
3. What can I book?
4. Why should I trust Preetha?
5. What is the next step?

Recommended homepage sections:

1. **Hero**
   - Brand name: Mindful Yogi
   - Short positioning line
   - Primary CTA: Book a Session
   - Secondary CTA: Explore Programmes

2. **Who This Is For**
   - Beginners
   - Busy adults seeking calm
   - People wanting gentle mobility and breath awareness
   - Women seeking supportive wellbeing practices
   - Parents, educators, or yoga teachers interested in Mindful Yogi products

3. **Featured Programmes**
   - Beginner Yoga Foundations
   - Stress Relief and Breathwork
   - Gentle Back-Care Yoga
   - Women’s Wellbeing / PCOS-Supportive Yoga
   - Private 1:1 Yoga
   - Yoga Philosophy Sessions

4. **Meet Preetha**
   - Short bio
   - Teaching style
   - Cultural/philosophical grounding
   - Training or certification details can be added once finalized

5. **How Booking Works**
   - Choose a session

   - Book a first session or send an enquiry

## Site Architecture

MVP pages:

- **Home**: overview, value proposition, featured services, booking CTA.
- **Work With Me**: session types, packages, and who each option is for.
- **Programmes**: goal-based offerings such as stress relief, back care, beginner yoga, and women’s wellbeing.
- **Book**: booking calendar, package selection, payment/confirmation flow.
- **Yoga Philosophy**: video series and written reflections.
- **Products**: Mindful Yogi cards/decks and links to Etsy or shop destination.
- **About**: Preetha’s story, teaching values, and training.
- **Contact**: enquiry form, email, social links.

Later pages:

- **Membership**: subscriptions, monthly themes, class library.
- **Blog / Reflections**: articles on practice, breath, philosophy, parenting, and wellbeing.
- **Resources**: downloadable guides, worksheets, class notes, or mini-practices.

## Services and Packages

Use practical, outcome-oriented names. The language should feel reassuring but must not promise medical outcomes.

### Beginner Yoga Foundations

For people new to yoga or returning after a break.

Include:

- basic postures;
- breath awareness;
- safe transitions;
- confidence-building;
- simple home practice.

Possible formats:

- single introductory class;
- 4-week beginner package;
- private 1:1 foundations session.

### Stress Relief and Breathwork

For adults seeking calmer routines, grounding, and everyday stress support.

Include:

- gentle movement;
- breath practices;
- relaxation;
- guided stillness;
- short practices for home use.

### Gentle Back-Care Yoga

For people seeking gentle movement, posture awareness, mobility, and relaxation for the back.

Important: do not claim to cure pain or treat injury.

### Women’s Wellbeing / PCOS-Supportive Yoga

For women seeking supportive yoga around stress, energy, body awareness, and hormonal wellbeing.

Important: describe this as complementary wellbeing support, not PCOS treatment.

### Private 1:1 Yoga

Personalized sessions for goals such as confidence, breathwork, mobility, stress relief, or building a regular practice.

Include:

- intake form;
- tailored session plan;
- optional follow-up notes;
- Google Meet option.

### Small Group Sessions

For families, friends, workplaces, or community groups.

Include:

- custom theme;
- online or local option if relevant;
- enquiry-based booking.

### Yoga Philosophy Sessions

For students interested in yoga beyond postures.

Possible topics:

- yoga beyond asana;
- breath and attention;
- practice in everyday life;
- Indian philosophical concepts explained accessibly.

## Booking, Calendar, Payment, and Google Meet Flow

Recommended MVP flow:

1. Visitor selects a service or package.
2. Visitor chooses a session type: single class, package, or discovery call.
3. Visitor chooses a date and time from a timezone-aware calendar.
4. Visitor enters name, email, phone/WhatsApp if needed, and a short intake note.
5. Visitor pays online or chooses manual payment if enabled.
6. Confirmation email is sent.
7. Google Calendar event is created.
8. Google Meet link is included automatically.
9. Reminder email is sent before the session.

Booking features to support:

- timezone clarity;
- buffer time between sessions;
- cancellation/reschedule policy;
- payment status;
- simple admin view;
- package tracking later.

Fastest integration path:

- Calendly + Stripe + Google Calendar + Google Meet.

More customizable path:

- Cal.com + Stripe + Google Calendar + Google Meet.

## Health and Safety Wording

Use careful wellbeing language.

Use:

- “supports wellbeing”;
- “gentle movement”;
- “breath awareness”;
- “mobility and relaxation”;
- “complementary practice”;
- “may support stress management.”

Avoid:

- “cures back pain”;
- “treats PCOS”;
- “reverses hormonal imbalance”;
- “guaranteed stress relief”;
- “medical therapy.”

Suggested general disclaimer:

> Mindful Yogi sessions are intended for general wellbeing, movement, breath awareness, and personal practice. They are not a substitute for medical diagnosis, treatment, or therapy. If you are pregnant, injured, in pain, managing a medical condition, or unsure whether yoga is suitable for you, please consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning.

Suggested PCOS-related note:

> PCOS-supportive yoga is offered as a gentle wellbeing practice that may support stress management, body awareness, and regular movement. It does not replace medical care or individualized advice from a healthcare professional.

Suggested back-care note:

> Back-care yoga focuses on gentle movement, breath, posture awareness, and relaxation. It is not a treatment for injury or severe pain. Please seek medical guidance if you have ongoing, acute, or unexplained pain.

## Suggested Technical Stack

Recommended MVP:

- **Framework:** Next.js
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Content:** Markdown/MDX for philosophy posts and service copy
- **Hosting:** Vercel or Netlify
- **Booking:** Calendly or Cal.com
- **Payments:** Stripe
- **Calendar:** Google Calendar
- **Video meetings:** Google Meet
- **Forms:** Formspree, Resend, or simple serverless form handling
- **Email:** Resend, Mailchimp, or Brevo later
- **Analytics:** Plausible, Fathom, or Google Analytics
- **Commerce MVP:** link out to Etsy
- **Commerce later:** Shopify Starter, Stripe Checkout, or custom storefront

Fastest launch stack:

> Next.js + Tailwind + Calendly + Stripe + Google Calendar + Etsy links.

More customizable stack:

> Next.js + Tailwind + Cal.com + Stripe + Google Calendar + MDX.
